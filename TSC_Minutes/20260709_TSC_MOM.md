## Drop of Ceph Ansible, Debian 12 and Yocto Scarthgap support
Moved to SEAPATH 2.0 following version of components are no longer supported:
- SEAPATH Scarthgap LTS in favor of SEAPATH Wrynose LTS
- SEAPATH Debian 12 in favor of SEAPATH Debian 13
- Ceph-ansible in favor of Cephadm

These components were maintened by SFL and RTE.
Voluntary companies are welcomed to offer support of these version.

TSC decision (vote):
- RTE: Bastien Desbos (absent)
- GE Vernova: David Macdonald (present)
- SFL: Eloi Bail (present)
- Schneider: Camille Bloch (present)
- Welotec: Jan Hille (present)
All members voted the drop of Ceph Ansible, Debian 12 and Yocto Scarthgap.
Approved by the majority.
